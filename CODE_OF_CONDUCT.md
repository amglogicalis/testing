# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code-of-conduct/) in all our interactions with the project.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment for contributors:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Telling offensive jokes, comments, or stories
- Repeatedly asking for support or help
- Insulting or demeaning others
- Publicly shamefully attacking another person in a private conversation

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Project contributors who violate these rules may be asked to leave the project without warning.
